Transit is lonely. You share a train car with hundreds of strangers every morning, never knowing a name or where they're off to. You wait in the cold for a bus that's always somewhere else. And sometimes the bus comes for no one at all.

There's beauty in a bus running with no one inside. It's like a birdhouse nobody has moved into, or porch lights left on for a friend who bailed. A consistent opportunity and an afterthought.

In **Milton**, a driver on the **245** passes a sign at **Brook Rd @ Windsor Rd** sixteen times a day. In **10** years of counts, the MBTA hasn't counted anyone waiting there.
